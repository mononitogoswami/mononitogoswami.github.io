I’ve benefited from generous mentors, and I try to offer the same support to the students I work with.

I enjoy collaborating with students at all levels on open-ended research problems and helping them develop their own ideas into strong research projects. To explore working together, reach out.

Office hours. I’m happy to chat about research with students and fellow researchers. I’ve set aside Fridays, 4:00–5:00 PM Pacific Time for these conversations. Please email me to arrange a time. We can find another time if Fridays don’t work for you.
