I study reasoning and learning in AI systems, focusing on training and evaluating self-improving AI systems for safety-critical applications.

I also lead science for the AWS DevOps Agent, a self-improving software reliability engineering agent, serving thousands of customers worldwide.

Previously, I extended advances in language modeling to structured data, helping establish foundation models for time series through MOMENT and Chronos-2. I also dabbled in scalable evaluation, model selection, multimodality, weak supervision, mechanistic interpretability, and ML for healthcare and education.

I completed my Ph.D. in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Artur Dubrawski, and received the Robotics Institute Distinguished Dissertation Award. I have also worked at Google Research and AWS AI Labs.

I care deeply about mentoring and working with students on ambitious research problems. To talk research or explore working together, reach out.
